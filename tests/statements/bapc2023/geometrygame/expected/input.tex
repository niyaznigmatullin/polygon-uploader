
    The input consists of:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Four lines, each containing two integers $x$ and $y$ ($0\leq x,y\leq 10^9$), the coordinates of a point.
    \end{itemize}
    The positive x-axis is oriented to the right and the positive y-axis is oriented upwards.

    The four points are distinct,
    form a convex quadrilateral shape (i.e.~all interior angles are strictly less than $180^\circ$),
    and are given in clockwise order.
