You are standing on a point with integer coordinates $(x_s, y_s)$. 
You want to walk to the point with integer coordinates $(x_t, y_t)$. 
To do this, you can walk along a sequence of line segments. 
But there is a swimming pool in your way. The swimming pool is an axis aligned 
rectangle whose lower left corner is on the point $(x_l, y_l)$ and the upper right 
corner is on the point $(x_r, y_r)$. You cannot ever cross the swimming pool, 
not even on the border. However, it is dark and you do not know the 
coordinates $(x_l, y_l)$ and $(x_r, y_r)$. Instead, you threw a rock into the pool which 
revealed that the point $(x_p, y_p)$ is in the pool (or on the boundary).

Find a way to walk from the start to the end point along a sequence of line segments, 
so that you never cross the swimming pool.
