
    The input consists of:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item One line with two integers $n$ and $w$ ($1\leq n\leq 10^5$, $1\leq w\leq 10^9$),
            the number of mills and the amount of wheat you have, in kilograms.
        \item $n$ lines, each with two integers $p$ and $t$ ($1\leq p, t\leq 10^9$),
            describing a mill that can process $p$ kilograms of wheat per hour, located $t$ hours away.
    \end{itemize}
