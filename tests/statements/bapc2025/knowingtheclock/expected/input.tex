
    The input consists of:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item One line with two integers $h$ and $m$ ($0 \leq h, m \leq 359$),
            the exact clockwise angle that the hour hand is from 12 o'clock and
            the exact clockwise angle that the minute hand is from 12 o'clock.
            Both angles are given in degrees.
    \end{itemize}
