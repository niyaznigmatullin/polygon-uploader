\problemname{Jump}

\illustration{0.3}{metal.jpg}{
  Ballsout at Metal Frenzy Open Air 2022, image by S. Bollmann, \href{https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ballsout\_Metal\_Frenzy\_2022\_05.jpg}{commons.wikimedia.org}.
}

The Unsightly Halfwits are putting on a wild concert in their natural habitat of Húsavík.
Their lead singer, Sveinn “Springheel” Jónsson, is leaping about the stage with vigorous energy, 
turning every note into a spectacle.
But keeping in rhythm while bouncing so chaotically is no easy task, and you can’t help but wonder 
how long he can keep this up.

Sveinn starts with an energy level of~$E$.
The song plays a beat exactly every $N$~milliseconds.
His first jump launches at time~$0$, perfectly in sync with the music. As long as his energy~$E$ is strictly positive ($E>0$), each jump lasts exactly $E$~milliseconds.
When his energy runs out with $E=0$, he collapses and the show is over. If he still has energy after landing, he immediately springs up again for the next jump.
When Sveinn lands exactly on a beat, he gains~$+1$ energy from sheer enjoyment.
But if he’s even slightly off-beat, his energy drops by~$1$.

\begin{Input}
    The input consists of:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item One line with two integers $N, E$, the number of milliseconds between beats and the initial energy level of the lead singer. These will satisfy $1 \leq N \leq 3 \cdot 10^5$ and $1 \leq E \leq 10^9$.
    \end{itemize}
\end{Input}

\begin{Output}
  Output a single integer, the number of milliseconds that
    will pass before Sveinn stops jumping. 
    If he never stops, instead print \texttt{infinity}.
\end{Output}
