Your social media company ConnectHub prides itself on how easily 
information spreads through its network.

Accounts can share posts directly with their followers using the 
share button, or with the accounts they follow by posting in the comments. 
ConnectHub is famous for the virality of its users: a post from any account 
was able to reach every other account, possibly through a chain of shares and comments.

But disaster struck: a database mishap wiped out all records of who follows whom. 
One piece of data may have survived, though — for each account, you have the number 
of its followers and the number of accounts it follows.
Maybe even those numbers have been corrupted, but it's all you have.

Your task is to rebuild the missing database so that these counts are correct, 
and ConnectHub once again has full virality.
