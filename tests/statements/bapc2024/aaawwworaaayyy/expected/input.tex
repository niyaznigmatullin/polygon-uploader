
    The input consists of:
    \vspace{-0.5em}% Ugly negative vspace to make the Input section fit on one page
    \begin{itemize}
        \item One line with three integers $n$, $m$, and $r$
            ($2\leq n\leq100$, $1\leq m\leq100$, $1\leq r\leq n$),
            the number of teams, the number of problems,
            and the rank of your favourite team in the frozen scoreboard.

        \item $n$ lines with $m$ characters,
            each character being either `\texttt{A}', `\texttt{R}', `\texttt{P}', or `\texttt{N}',
            indicating that a team's submission is either accepted, rejected, pending, or nothing.
            The teams are sorted by their rank in the frozen scoreboard,
            i.e.\ descending by number of accepted submissions in the frozen scoreboard,
            and further tie-breaking rules guarantee that all teams have a distinct rank.

        \item For each pending submission, one line with two strings,
            containing the audience chant at the reveal of the pending submission, in chronological order.
            The first string is ``\texttt{Ooohhh...}'' and the second string is either ``\texttt{Aaawww...}''
            or ``\texttt{Aaayyy!!!}'', with an additional `\texttt{y}' for each team that is passed when rising in the ranking.
    \end{itemize}
