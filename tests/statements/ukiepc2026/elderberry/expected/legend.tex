

Jeanne and her son Semi-Demi-John are engaged
in the elderberry-wine making business.

The recipe requires, aside from a closely-guarded secret quantity of the letter
$r$, strict proportions of $12$ parts fresh elderberries, $12$ parts brewing
sugar, and $40$ parts water.
As long as one or more ingredients are lacking, the team will place an order for
as much extra as needed to make up the weight, and start again.

For instance:
\begin{itemize}
  \item
        If they have $18$kg of elderberries, $9$kg of sugar, and $100$kg
        of water, they can use $9$kg of elderberries, all $9$kg of the sugar, and $30$kg
        of the water and then they are stuck.
  \item
        They must then order $9$kg more of sugar in order to proceed and use
        $9$kg, $9$kg, and $30$kg again.
  \item
        After this they must order $12$kg each of elderberries and sugar in order
        to finally use $12$kg, $12$kg, and $40$kg of elderberries, sugar, and water
        respectively.
\end{itemize}
