

\begin{itemize}
  \item The first line of input contains the number of vertices of the cheese piece, $n$ ($3 \le n \le 10\,000$),
        and the integer $x$-coordinate of the finish line in metres, $m$ ($1 \le m \le 10^6$).

  \item The next $n$ lines of input contain the $x$ and $y$ coordinates of cheese vertices, in anti-clockwise order,
        $x_i$ and $y_i$ ($0 \le x_i, y_i \le 10\,000$).
\end{itemize}

No three adjacent vertices on the shape are collinear and at least one vertex has $y = 0$.
The cheese has not yet crossed the line.
