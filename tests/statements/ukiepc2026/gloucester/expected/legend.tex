

A piece of cheese with polygonal cross-sectional area is rolling along a flat
surface as part of a major sporting event.

The piece of cheese rotates clockwise continuously, pivoting around the vertex
with lowest $y$-coordinate and, in case of a tie, greatest x-coordinate.

\begin{figure}[!h]
\centering
\includegraphics[height=0.4\textwidth]{sample1.pdf}
\caption{Illustration of Sample Input 1.}
\end{figure}

The finish line is $m$ metres from the origin point and must be crossed at ground level,
meaning that one of the vertices on the ground is strictly past the line.

How many revolutions does the piece of cheese make in order for at least one
part touching the ground to be strictly past the line?
