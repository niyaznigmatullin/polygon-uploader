\problemname{Dire Strait}

Mickey has been enjoying sailing on the local lake, but now it's the end of
the day and it's time to go back to dock to return the rental boat on time.

% Mickey is playing his favourite real-time strategy game, Age Of War Crimes,
% against an AI opponent. The game is very hard and Mickey is not a good
% strategist, so he is losing.
% 
% The first incursion was highly successful, scoring many points. However, the
% AI responded by threatening to close a crucial shipping channel while
% Mickey's flagship, the \emph{Golden Comb}, is still docked at a port inside.

Find the shortest path from its
current location to the one remaining egress point over water, staying within or
passing along the coastline of the lake.

The lake is represented as a simple, potentially
concave, polygon having vertices at integer coordinates, and with no
$3$ consecutive vertices being collinear.

A triangulation of the lake into $(n-2)$ pieces is also provided.

\section*{Input}

\begin{itemize}
  \item The number of vertices in the polygon representing the lake,
        $n$ ($3 \le n \le 100\,000$), and the indices of the two vertices between
        which to travel, $a$ and $b$ ($1 \le a, b \le n; a \ne b$).
  \item $n$ further lines, each giving integer coordinates $x_i$ $y_i$ of the $i$th
        vertex of the polygon ($-10^9 \le x, y \le 10^9$) in anti-clockwise
        order.
  \item $(n-2)$ further lines giving a triangulation of the lake.
        Each line specifies the $1$-based indices of three vertices $u$ $v$ $w$ (in anti-clockwise order).
\end{itemize}

The given triangles correspond to the polygon, thus cover the lake
without any self-overlaps, holes, or other hazards.

\section*{Output}

Output the shortest distance from point $a$ to point $b$ without
exiting the lake.

The output must be accurate to an absolute or relative error of $10^{-6}$.
