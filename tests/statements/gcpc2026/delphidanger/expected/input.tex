
The input consists of:
\begin{itemize}
	\item One line with two integers $n$ and $m$ ($2\leq n\leq 2 \cdot 10^5$, $1\leq m\leq4 \cdot 10^5$), the number of caravans and the number of prophecies.
	\item $m$ lines, each with three integers $u$, $v$, and $t$ ($1\leq u, v\leq n$, $u\neq v$, $0\leq t\leq 10^9$), describing a prophecy:
	the strategies of caravans $u$ and $v$ will cause them to steer in the same direction exactly $t$ times, and then steer in opposite directions.
\end{itemize}
It is guaranteed that each pair of caravans appears at most once.
