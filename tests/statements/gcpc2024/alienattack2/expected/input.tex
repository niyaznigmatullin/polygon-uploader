
    The input consists of:
    \begin{itemize}
    \item One line with two integers $n$ and $m$ ($1\leq n\leq 2 \cdot 10^5$, $0\leq m\leq 2 \cdot 10^5$), the number of people and the total number of friendships between them.
    \item $m$ lines, each with two integers $i$ and $j$ ($1\leq i < j\leq n$), denoting a friendship between persons $i$ and $j$.
    \end{itemize}
    The people are numbered from $1$ to $n$. It is guaranteed that no friendship is listed multiple times.
