
    The input consists of:
	\begin{itemize}
		\item One line with two integers $n$ and $m$ ($2 \leq n \leq 10^5$, $0 \leq m \leq 10^5$), the number of train stations and train connections, respectively.
		\item $m$ lines, each with two integers $x$ and $y$ ($1 \leq x, y \leq n$, $x \neq y$), denoting a direct train connection from train station $x$ to train station $y$.
		\item One line with two integers $a$ and $b$ ($1 \leq a,b \leq n$, $a \neq b$), the train stations you and Hannah\xspace start from, respectively.
	\end{itemize}
	It is guaranteed that no train connection is listed multiple times.
