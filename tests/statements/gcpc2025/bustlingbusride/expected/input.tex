
    The input consists of:
    \begin{itemize}
      \item One line with four integers $n$, $b$, $r$, and $w$ ($1\leq n, b \leq 10^5$, $1\leq r, w \leq 10^6$), the number of passengers, the number of bus stops, the time between the arrival of two buses at the university, and the delay for exiting and entering.
      \item One line with $b$ integers $d_{i}$ ($1 \le d_{i}, \sum d_{i} \leq 10^6$), the travel time from the $(i-1)$th bus stop to the $i$th bus stop (the $0$th bus stop is the university).
      \item One line with $n$ integers $t_{i}$ ($1 \leq t_{i} \leq b$), indicating that the destination of the $i$th person in line is the $t_i$th bus stop.
    \end{itemize}
    All times are given in seconds.
